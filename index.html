Meet Courtney

Started by a dog person, for dog people

Paws, People & Moore began the way most good things do: with a dog who deserved better than what the supermarket aisle had to offer. Courtney started sourcing treats and toys for her own pack, friends started asking where she got them, and a small business was born.

Today it's still small, still local, and still run by someone who will happily talk about your dog for as long as you like. Every product is something she'd give her own.
